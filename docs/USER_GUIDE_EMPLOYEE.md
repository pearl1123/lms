# kaBAGA Academy — Employee / Student User Guide

**For:** Learners (employees taking training)  
**System:** kaBAGA Academy LMS  
**Organization:** Lung Center of the Philippines

This guide explains how to sign in, enroll in courses, complete lessons, take assessments, and get your certificate.

---

## 1. Getting Started

### Sign in
1. Open the kaBAGA Academy website.
2. Enter your **Employee ID** and **password**.
3. Click **Login**.
4. Optional: check **Remember me** on a trusted computer.

### New account (first time)
1. Click **Register**.
2. Enter your **Employee ID** (must be an active HRMIS employee).
3. Create a password and complete registration.
4. Sign in with your new account.

### Forgot password
1. On the login page, click **Forgot password**.
2. Follow the email link to set a new password.
3. Sign in with the new password.

### If your account is locked
After too many wrong password attempts, the system locks your account for a short time. Contact ETD or your LMS administrator to unlock it.

---

## 2. Your Home (Dashboard)

After login you will see your **Dashboard**. Here you can:
- Continue the course you were last working on
- See how many courses you enrolled in and completed
- View recent certificates and activity
- Go quickly to **My Courses** or the **Course Catalog**

---

## 3. Find and Join a Course

### Browse the catalog
1. Click **Course Catalog** in the left menu.
2. Use search or category filters to find a course.
3. Click a course to read the details and module list.

### Request enrollment
1. Open the course page.
2. Click **Enroll** (or the enrollment button shown).
3. Select a batch/cohort if asked.
4. Wait for approval from your instructor or ETD.

### Course invitation
If you receive an invitation:
- Open the course or notification.
- Choose **Accept** or **Decline**.

### Face-to-face courses
Some courses are on-site. The course page may show schedule, venue, and an advisory notice.

---

## 4. My Courses

1. Click **My Courses** (or **My Learning**) in the menu.
2. You will see all courses you are enrolled in, with progress.
3. Click a course to open it, or use **Continue / Resume** to return where you left off.

---

## 5. Taking a Course (Lessons)

Open a course, then open a module. Complete modules in order when the course requires it.

### Video lessons
1. Watch the video.
2. Answer **checkpoint** questions when they appear (you must answer to continue).
3. Finish watching the full video.
4. When the video ends, you may be asked to take the **post-assessment**.

### PDF / slides
1. Use **Previous** and **Next** to move page by page.
2. Use zoom (+/−) or rotate if the page is hard to read.
3. Go through every page; you cannot skip to the end to finish early.
4. Your last page is saved so you can resume later.

### Audio, links, or recordings
Open the content as shown for that module.

### Progress
A progress bar shows how far you are in the module (checkpoints, video finished, assessment passed).

---

## 6. Assessments (Quizzes)

### Types you may see
- **Pre-assessment** — before or at the start of a lesson (sometimes optional)
- **Post-assessment** — after the lesson (often required to finish the module)
- **Video checkpoints** — short questions during a video

### How to take a quiz
1. Click **Take Assessment** (from the module sidebar or Assessments menu).
2. Answer all required questions.
3. For essays, type your answer and/or upload a file if asked.
4. You can **Save & return later** if you need to stop mid-quiz.
5. Click **Submit** when finished.

### After you submit
- You will see your score and whether you passed.
- You can **review your answers**.
- If you passed and the course is finished, you can continue to **mark the module complete** and get your **certificate**.
- If you did not pass, you may **retake** (rules depend on the course).

### Tip
On video modules, finish watching the video before the post-assessment unlocks.

---

## 7. Finish a Module and Earn a Certificate

1. Complete all lesson requirements (watch/read, checkpoints, post-assessment).
2. Click **Mark as Complete**.
3. Repeat for remaining modules.
4. When the whole course is done, your **certificate** is issued.
5. Go to **Certificates** to view or download it as PDF.

You can also verify a certificate using its certificate code on the verification page.

---

## 8. Certificates

1. Click **Certificates** in the menu.
2. Open a certificate to view it.
3. Download the PDF if you need a copy for your records.

---

## 9. My Notes

1. Click **My Notes** in the menu.
2. Create notes while learning (or from the notes page).
3. You can pin favorites, edit, delete, and export notes.
4. Use the return link to go back to the related lesson when available.

---

## 10. Announcements

1. Click **Announcements** in the menu.
2. A red badge shows unread messages.
3. Open items to read them; mark as read when done.

---

## 11. My Profile

1. Click **My Profile**.
2. Update contact details or bio if allowed.
3. Upload a profile photo.
4. Change your password (enter current password, then new password).

---

## 12. Quick Path (Full Training)

1. Sign in  
2. Find a course in **Course Catalog** and enroll  
3. Wait for approval  
4. Open **My Courses** → start modules  
5. Complete lessons and assessments  
6. Mark modules complete  
7. Download your certificate  

---

## Need Help?

- Locked account or login problems → contact ETD / LMS administrator  
- Course content or enrollment questions → contact your instructor or ETD  
- Certificate not appearing → confirm all modules and post-assessments are completed and passed
