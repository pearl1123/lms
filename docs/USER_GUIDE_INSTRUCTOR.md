# kaBAGA Academy — Instructor User Guide

**For:** Instructors / Teachers  
**System:** kaBAGA Academy LMS  
**Organization:** Lung Center of the Philippines

This guide explains how to monitor your classes, approve enrollments, review assessments, and support learners.

---

## 1. Getting Started

### Sign in
1. Open the kaBAGA Academy website.
2. Enter your **Employee ID** and **password**.
3. Click **Login**.

### Forgot password
Use **Forgot password** on the login page and follow the email link.

### If your account is locked
Contact your LMS administrator or ETD to reset the login lock.

---

## 2. Instructor Dashboard

After login, your **Dashboard** shows:
- Number of courses assigned to you
- Active students
- Pending enrollment requests
- Average completion
- Shortcuts to **My Courses** and **Enrollment Requests**
- Recent activity and items that need attention

Use this page each day to see who is waiting for approval and how your courses are progressing.

---

## 3. My Courses (Your Teaching Load)

1. Click **My Courses** in the left menu.
2. You will see courses assigned to you.
3. Open a course to view details, modules, and learner progress.
4. Use course actions to continue editing (if you have edit access) or to review the course outline.

**Tip:** Your menu label may say **My Courses** or similar, depending on system settings.

---

## 4. Enrollment Requests (Approve Learners)

Learners must often be approved before they can start.

1. Click **Enrollment Requests** in the menu (or use the Dashboard shortcut).
2. Review the pending list (learner name, course, date).
3. Click **Approve** or **Reject**.
4. The learner is notified of the result.

Only pending requests for your courses (or courses you manage) should appear in your list.

---

## 5. Course Catalog (Browse Available Courses)

1. Click **Course Catalog**.
2. Search or filter courses.
3. Open a course to review how it appears to learners.

If you also have course management rights, you may create or edit courses from **Manage Courses** (see section 10).

---

## 6. Assessments — Review and Grade

### View assessments
1. Click **Assessments** in the menu.
2. Open an assessment linked to your courses.

### Review submissions
1. Open **Review** for an assessment.
2. See who has submitted and their scores.
3. Open a learner’s attempt to view answers.

### Grade essays
1. Open the learner’s grading page.
2. Score essay answers that are pending review.
3. Save grades.
4. The learner’s final result updates when grading is complete.

### Integrity / analytics (if available)
Managers and instructors with access can open integrity analytics for selected assessments.

---

## 7. Supporting Learners Through a Course

Help learners follow this path:

1. Enroll → you **approve**  
2. Open modules in order  
3. Watch video / read PDF / slides  
4. Answer video checkpoints  
5. Finish the video (post-assessment unlocks after the video ends)  
6. Take and **pass** the post-assessment  
7. Mark module complete  
8. Finish all modules → **certificate** is issued  

### Common learner questions

| Question | What to tell them |
|----------|-------------------|
| “Post-assessment is locked” | Finish watching the full video first. |
| “I can’t mark complete” | Pass the post-assessment and finish all module requirements. |
| “No certificate yet” | All modules must be completed and all required post-assessments passed. |
| “Account locked” | Ask Admin/ETD to use **Reset login lock** in User Management. |
| “I failed the quiz” | They may retake (rules depend on the course; some require restarting the full course). |

---

## 8. Certificates

1. Click **Certificates**.
2. View certificates related to your courses / learners (as allowed by your role).
3. Learners download their own certificates from their account after course completion.

Certificates are usually issued automatically when the learner finishes the course.

### Announcements

- Unread items show a badge on the menu.

### My Profile
- Update contact info and photo.
- Change your password.

## 10. Manage Courses *(if your account allows it)*

Some instructors can create or edit course content.

**Full step-by-step guide:** see [`USER_GUIDE_CREATE_COURSE.md`](USER_GUIDE_CREATE_COURSE.md)  
*(Create draft → Overview → Modules → Assessments → Access → Instructors → Certificates → Batches → Publish)*

### Short version
1. Open **Manage Courses** → **Create Course**.
2. Enter title, modality, and categories → create the **draft**.
3. In the course workspace:
   - **Overview** — finish course details  
   - **Modules** — add lessons (video, PDF, slides, audio, links); set weight; drag to reorder  
   - Add **Pre / Post assessments** and **video checkpoints** from each module  
   - **Access** — enrollment / visibility (optional)  
   - **Instructors** — assign teachers  
   - **Certificates** — prefix and signatories (optional)  
   - **Batches** — cohorts (optional)  
4. **Save**, then **Publish** so learners can enroll.

### Notes
- If learners are already enrolled, adding / deleting / reordering modules may be locked.
- Publish only when content and assessments are ready.

## 11. My Classes *(if shown)*

Some instructor accounts have a **My Classes** menu item for class-oriented views. Use it if available; otherwise use **My Courses** and **Enrollment Requests**.

---

## 12. Daily Instructor Checklist

- [ ] Check **Dashboard** for pending enrollments and alerts  
- [ ] Approve or reject **Enrollment Requests**  
- [ ] Review **Assessments** that need essay grading  
- [ ] Check learner progress on your courses in **My Courses**  
- [ ] Respond to learner issues (locked quiz, retake, certificate)  

---

## Need Help?

- Access or login issues → LMS Administrator / ETD  
- Course publishing or structure → ETD / System Admin  
- Certificate problems → confirm learner completed all modules and passed post-assessments
